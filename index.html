G3 King Fight

A turn-based battle of kings

How to Play

⚔️ Two kings battle each other in turns.

❤️ Each king starts with 100 HP.

🎮 On your turn, choose an action:

  • Attack – Deal 10–25 damage to your opponent.
  • Heavy Strike – Deal 20–40 damage but costs 5 MP.
  • Guard – Reduce incoming damage by 50% next turn.
  • Heal – Restore 15–25 HP (costs 10 MP).

💧 Each king starts with 50 MP, regenerating 5 MP per turn.

👑 The last king standing wins!
